Sources
• ~330 billion cells replaced per day — Sender & Milo, Nature Medicine (2021): https://wis-wander.weizmann.ac.il/node/11205 (≈3.8 million/s is our arithmetic)
• ~60,000 miles of blood vessels; ~2,000 gallons (7,500+ L) of blood circulated a day — Cleveland Clinic: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/21640-blood-vessels · https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/circulatory-and-cardiovascular-system
• ~100,000 heartbeats/day = our arithmetic at 70 beats/min (70 × 60 × 24)
• 17,000+ breaths/day, 2,000+ gallons of air, ~1,500 miles of airways, tennis-court surface, ~6 L lung capacity — American Lung Association: https://www.lung.org/blog/how-your-lungs-work
• Right lung 3 lobes, left lung 2 — Cleveland Clinic: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/8960-lungs
• 10 kg fat → 8.4 kg exhaled as CO2 — Meerman & Brown, BMJ 2014;349:g7257: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2014/12/141216212041.htm
• Kidneys filter ~150 quarts/day, 1–2 quarts urine, ~1 million nephrons each — NIDDK: https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidneys-how-they-work
• Red blood cells live ~120 days — Cleveland Clinic
• ~86 billion neurons, ~3 lb brain — Cleveland Clinic: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/22638-brain
• Fastest nerve signals 70–120 m/s (~270 mph) — Tim Welsh, University of Toronto (via EarthSky): https://earthsky.org/human-world/what-is-the-speed-of-thought/
• 14–17 blinks a minute — Cleveland Clinic: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/blinking
